# Parking and Transport

Seabreeze Hotels is a fictional company created for a software demonstration.

## Parking

### Harbor View

The underground garage is open 24 hours. Self-parking costs 32 dollars per night, and valet parking costs 45 dollars per night. Motorcycles park for 16 dollars per night.

The height limit is 2.0 metres. Larger vehicles cannot enter the garage and must use the public lot on Quay Street.

Six electric vehicle chargers are available at 0.35 dollars per kWh. They cannot be reserved.

### Old Town

Old Town has no parking of its own, because the historic centre is closed to traffic. Guests may stop outside the hotel for 15 minutes to unload.

Guests park at the Rope Walk Garage, 400 metres away, for a discounted 18 dollars per night. Ask reception for a voucher, as the normal price is 30 dollars. The height limit there is 1.9 metres.

### Dune Retreat

Parking is free. Cars stay at the car park beside reception, and golf carts take guests and luggage to the villas. There are no electric vehicle chargers on the island.

## Reaching Sandhaven Island

Dune Retreat can only be reached by ferry from Port Calloway. The crossing takes 40 minutes and carries both cars and foot passengers.

The last ferry leaves at 21:30 from April to October and at 19:00 from November to March. A car and driver pay 38 dollars each way, and foot passengers pay 9 dollars. Ferry tickets are not included in room rates.

A free shuttle meets every ferry at the Sandhaven terminal. Book it at the time of your reservation if you are travelling with more than four people.

## Airport Transfers

### Harbor View Shuttle

An airport shuttle runs every 30 minutes from 05:00 to 23:00. It costs 18 dollars per person each way, and children under 6 ride free.

### Old Town

Old Town has no shuttle. Guests can take the Harbor View shuttle and walk 10 minutes, or take the number 7 tram.

### Taxis

A taxi from Calloway Airport costs about 42 dollars to either Port Calloway hotel.

## Getting Around

Bicycles are free for guests at Dune Retreat. Harbor View rents bicycles for 15 dollars per day. Old Town does not offer bicycles.
