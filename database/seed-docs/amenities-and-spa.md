# Amenities and Spa

Seabreeze Hotels is a fictional company created for a software demonstration.

## Swimming Pools

### Harbor View Rooftop Pool

The rooftop pool is open from 07:00 to 22:00. It is reserved for adults after 19:00.

### Old Town

Old Town has no pool. Guests may use the Harbor View rooftop pool for free during their stay.

### Dune Retreat Pool

The outdoor pool is open from 08:00 to 20:00. It is heated from April to October and closed from December to February.

## Spa

### Tide Spa at Harbor View

Tide Spa is open from 10:00 to 20:00 and closed on Mondays. Harbor View guests use the sauna and steam room for free. Old Town guests get a 20 percent discount on treatments, but pay 15 dollars to use the sauna. Non-guests can buy a day pass for 35 dollars.

A 60-minute massage costs 95 dollars.

### Dune Spa at Dune Retreat

Dune Spa is open every day from 09:00 to 19:00. A 60-minute massage costs 110 dollars. The spa is for hotel guests only.

### Spa Rules

Guests under 16 cannot book treatments, and the sauna is for guests aged 18 and over.

Treatments must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance. Later cancellations are charged at 50 percent of the price.

## Fitness

The Harbor View gym is open 24 hours with your room key. The Old Town gym is open from 06:00 to 22:00. Dune Retreat has no gym, but offers free beach yoga on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 08:00.

## Dining

### Breakfast

| Property | Hours | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harbor View | 06:30 to 10:30 | 24 dollars |
| Old Town | 07:00 to 10:00 | Included in every rate |
| Dune Retreat | 07:30 to 11:00 | 28 dollars |

Children under 6 eat breakfast free at all properties.

### Restaurants

The Anchor restaurant at Harbor View serves dinner from 18:00 to 22:30 and is closed on Sundays. Old Town has no restaurant, only a bar that is open from 16:00 to midnight. The Driftwood at Dune Retreat serves lunch and dinner from 12:00 to 21:00 every day.

## Internet

Wi-Fi is free at all properties. Harbor View also offers a faster premium connection for 9 dollars per day. Wi-Fi at Dune Retreat works in the villas and at reception, but not on the beach.

## Laundry

Harbor View offers same-day laundry when items are handed in before 09:00. Old Town and Dune Retreat offer next-day laundry only.
