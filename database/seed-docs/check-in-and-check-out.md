# Check-in and Check-out

Seabreeze Hotels is a fictional company created for a software demonstration.

## Standard Times

| Property | Check-in from | Check-out by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harbor View | 15:00 | 11:00 |
| Old Town | 14:00 | 11:00 |
| Dune Retreat | 16:00 | 10:00 |

## Early Check-in

### Harbor View

Early check-in from 12:00 costs 30 dollars and can be booked up to 24 hours before arrival, subject to availability.

### Old Town

Early check-in from 12:00 is free whenever the room is ready. It cannot be guaranteed in advance.

### Dune Retreat

Early check-in is not offered, because villas are deep-cleaned between stays. You can leave luggage at reception from 10:00 and use the pool while you wait.

## Late Check-out

### Harbor View

Late check-out until 14:00 costs 35 dollars. Seabreeze Circle members receive it free of charge on Sundays.

### Old Town

Late check-out until 13:00 costs 25 dollars.

### Dune Retreat

Late check-out until 12:00 costs 50 dollars. It is never available on Saturdays, which are changeover days.

## Reception Hours

Reception at Harbor View and Old Town is open 24 hours a day. Reception at Dune Retreat is open from 07:00 to 23:00.

If you reach Dune Retreat after 23:00, your villa key is in the key safe at the gate. The code is sent by text message on the day of arrival.

## What to Bring

Every guest must show a government-issued photo ID at check-in. The person making the reservation must present the card used for booking.

The minimum check-in age is 18 at Harbor View and Old Town, and 21 at Dune Retreat. Guests under the minimum age may stay when they are accompanied by a parent or guardian.

## Luggage Storage

Luggage storage is free on the day of arrival and the day of departure at all properties. Harbor View charges 5 dollars per bag for storage on any other day.
